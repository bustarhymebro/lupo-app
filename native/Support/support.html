Lupo Support

Lupo is a habit tracker where you raise a wolf by keeping your real habits. Here is how to get help.

Contact

Email chleb@chlebholdings.com and we will get back to you.

Native iPhone app

This support draft describes the new native app. The existing website has different controls.

Move or protect your progress

Open Den, Settings & your data, then Export my data. Keep the JSON file somewhere you trust. On your other device choose Restore a backup. Restoring replaces that device's current progress after confirmation. Lupo has no automatic account synchronization in this version.

A goal does not appear today

Goal edits and new goals begin on the next game day, which starts at 4 a.m. local time. Check the goal's weekday schedule in Today. Past records keep the wording and schedule that applied at the time.

I tapped a goal by mistake

Use Undo last check-in immediately, before taking another action. This removes the completion and its reward. A rest day must be selected before earning goal, trail or focus rewards.

My wolf is hungry

Complete a goal, a guided-trail step or a focus session to earn a meal. Each meal restores nourishment. Growth and discoveries are kept after missed days, and you never need to purchase food or recovery.

Reminders are not showing

Open Den, Settings & your data, Reminder settings. Enable reminders and choose a time. Allow notifications in your device settings. Reminders skip completed days, rest days and days without goals. They cover the next two weeks and refresh when you open Lupo. System Focus and notification settings can delay or hide delivery.

Restore Lupo Plus

Open Explore Lupo Plus and choose Restore purchases using the Apple Account that bought the one-time unlock. A pending or unverified purchase does not grant access. Restoring Plus does not restore your wolf's progress; use your exported backup for that.

Guided trails

One step per game day earns a meal. There is no deadline to finish a trail. Starting another trail preserves completed steps and notes in the trail journal. Notes remain readable without Plus.

Reset local progress

Open Den, Settings & your data, then Delete my data and start again. Confirm only if you want to remove this device's local wolf and progress. Export a backup first if you want to keep them.
