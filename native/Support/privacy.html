Privacy Policy

Native app policy draft for release review. Updated November 8, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Chleb Holdings LLC ("Chleb Holdings", "we", "us") handles information in connection with the Lupo app and website ("Lupo", the "Service"). By using Lupo you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Who we are

Lupo is operated by Chleb Holdings LLC, based in Tampa, Florida, United States. You can reach us at chleb@chlebholdings.com.

2. Native app information

The native iPhone app stores your wolf, goal titles and schedules, completion history, focus sessions, guided-trail notes and reminder preferences in a local file on your device. The app does not send that file to us. This version has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK or developer-operated synchronization service.

Optional reminders are scheduled locally through Apple's notification system. Reminder text can include a goal title and may be visible on your lock screen according to your device settings. Reminders are silent and can be disabled in the app or in system settings.

Optional purchases are processed by Apple. The app reads verified StoreKit transactions to unlock purchased features. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple processes purchase information under its own policies.

3. Backups, sharing and support

You can export a JSON backup containing your app data, including private notes and goal titles. Where that file goes depends on the destination you choose. Anyone with the exported file may be able to read it. An exported backup is not encrypted by Lupo. Device or operating-system backups may also include app data according to your settings.

Sharing progress opens the system share interface and includes the wolf's name and bond count; it does not include private goal text or trail notes. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send. Please omit private notes unless necessary for your request.

4. Website services

The separate website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process standard request logs. If you voluntarily join its waitlist, Formsubmit delivers your email address to us. These website functions are separate from the native app's local progress. We do not sell personal information or include third-party advertising trackers in the native app.

5. Screen Time and focus

This native version does not request Family Controls authorization or read device Screen Time. The focus timer measures elapsed time; it does not verify that you stayed off other apps. Goals and guided-trail steps are self-reported.

6. Storage and deletion

Native app data remains on your device until you delete it using Den, Settings & your data, Delete my data and start again, or remove the app. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy. Previously exported files and system backups are managed separately by you. Deleting local progress does not revoke an Apple purchase; a purchased Plus unlock can be restored with the same Apple Account.

Waitlist and support information is kept only as needed for the request or applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information you have sent us.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. Because most data stays on your device, you can exercise much of this yourself through the reset and clear-data controls. For the waitlist email or any other request, contact us at chleb@chlebholdings.com and we will respond as required by applicable law, including the GDPR and the CCPA where they apply.

8. Children

Lupo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International users

We are based in the United States. If you use Lupo from outside the United States, any information sent to us (such as a waitlist email) is processed in the United States.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date above. Significant changes will be reflected here, and continued use after an update means you accept the new policy.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy go to chleb@chlebholdings.com.
